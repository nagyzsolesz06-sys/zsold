All my money in one place, always up to date.

Zsold is a personal finance and portfolio tracker I designed and built. It brings bank accounts, cash, ETFs and crypto into one dashboard, updates prices and MNB exchange rates on its own, and calculates every return in forints.

zsold.app

Everything my Excel tracker did, without the manual work.

Zsold started as an Excel workbook with Power Query. The web version keeps its logic and adds what a spreadsheet cannot do well.

Returns in forints, calculated properly.

Every trade is converted at the MNB rate of its own date, cost basis follows FIFO, and the currency effect is shown separately from how the asset itself performed.

  • ETF and bitcoin prices refresh every hour
  • Full price history for every holding
  • Each asset keeps its own colour across the app

Spending logged the way you would say it.

Type one sentence in Hungarian. A rule-based parser picks out the dates, amounts, accounts and categories, learns from earlier entries, and lets you check everything before it is saved.

tegnap yesterday kártyával by card kávé coffee

Every forint accounted for.

Account balances are rebuilt from every income, expense, transfer and trade, across forint, euro, dollar and franc accounts, so they can be checked against the bank down to the last forint.

The monthly view shows where the money came from and where it went.

Privacy modeOne tap hides every amount; percentages and market prices stay visible.
Recurring itemsFixed income or bills are suggested when they fall due.
Works on any screenA sidebar on desktop, a tab bar on the phone, installable as an app.
Hungarian tax ratesDividend and social contribution rates kept by year.

A small data pipeline behind a simple screen.

No servers to maintain. A scheduled job collects the data, a hosted Postgres database stores it with row-level security, and a lightweight web app reads it.

From a spreadsheet to a product I use every day.

I track my ETF portfolio and my spending closely, and for a while an Excel workbook did the job. It pulled MNB rates with Power Query and calculated my returns, but logging every coffee on a laptop was tedious, and I could not check anything from my phone.

So I turned it into a web app. I defined what it should do, designed the screens and checked every number against the original workbook, and I built it with AI coding tools. It now runs on its own domain and updates itself every hour.
