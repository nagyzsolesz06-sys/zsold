Features

Everything my Excel tracker did, without the manual work.

Zsold started as an Excel workbook with Power Query. The web version keeps its logic and adds what a spreadsheet cannot do well.

Live market data

A scheduled job fetches ETF and bitcoin prices and the official MNB exchange rates every hour, and keeps the full price history.

Returns in forints, done right

Cost basis uses FIFO, every trade is converted at the MNB rate of its own date, and the currency effect is shown separately from the asset return.

Accounts that reconcile

Balances are rebuilt from every income, expense, transfer and trade, so they can be checked against the bank down to the forint.

Smart expense entry in plain Hungarian

Type a sentence the way you would say it. A rule-based parser picks out dates, amounts, accounts and categories, and learns from earlier entries.

tegnap kínai kaja 2500 kártyával, kávé 650
becomes two entries
Yesterday · Restaurant, coffee · CardChinese food · 2 500 Ft
Yesterday · Restaurant, coffee · CardCoffee · 650 Ft

Privacy mode and recurring items

One tap hides every amount while percentages stay visible. Fixed items such as a scholarship are suggested when they are due.

How it works

A small data pipeline behind a simple screen.

No servers to maintain. A scheduled job collects the data, a hosted Postgres database stores it with row-level security, and a lightweight web app reads it.

Why I built it

From a spreadsheet to a product I use every day.

I track my ETF portfolio and my spending closely, and for a while an Excel workbook did the job. It pulled MNB rates with Power Query and calculated my returns, but logging every coffee on a laptop was tedious, and I could not check anything from my phone.

So I turned it into a web app. I defined what it should do, designed the screens and checked every number against the original workbook, and I built it with AI coding tools. It now runs on its own domain and updates itself every hour.
